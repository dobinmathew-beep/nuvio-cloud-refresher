Pick your perfect mix

Choose each language’s movies and series independently. One language or all four—it’s your evening.

02

Home releases, verified

Confirmed OTT dates plus dated DVD/Blu-ray movie editions. Theatrical-only and future releases stay out.

03

Metadata that connects

IMDb first, with verified TMDB and TVmaze fallbacks. AIOMetadata handles the rich detail pages.

04

Posters with personality

RPDB posters where available, high-quality artwork fallbacks, title logos and background art. New episodes get a seven-day badge and move to the front in all four series catalogs. Confirmed upcoming episodes or seasons show their next release date.

05

A fresh check, every day

6 am and 6 pm Muscat, plus 1 am Thursday–Saturday. Failed work is checked again every 30 minutes.

06

One free companion

No catalog subscription. Playback comes from your existing stream add-ons; provider coverage may vary.
