Before you gift that chai.

Can I use GPay or PhonePe from India?

UPI is not enabled on this site yet. GPay’s Indian UPI flow and its card-wallet feature are different payment methods. Cross-border UPI needs an eligible provider and merchant approval; a normal Indian UPI link does not settle directly to an Oman bank account. If your card supports international payments, an approved card checkout may be an alternative.

Which currency will I pay in?

The prepared checkout uses Omani rials (OMR). If your account uses another currency, your bank or payment provider determines conversion and any fees. Review the final amount on the secure checkout page.

Will this unlock more catalogs or films?

No. Every catalog remains free. Support helps maintain the project; playback requires your own streaming add-ons and their available sources.

Will I be charged again next month?

No. This page is designed for one-time contributions. It does not create a recurring subscription.

What if a payment fails?

A payment is confirmed only by the gateway’s receipt, not by this website. Do not retry a pending transaction until you check its status with the provider. If something seems wrong, contact the provider or your bank; you can report a website problem through our feedback form without sharing payment details.
